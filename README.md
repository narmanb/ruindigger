# RUIN DIGGER

*Break the ground. Wake what lies below. Get out richer.*

A side-view destructible-terrain mining roguelite for **Godot 4.6** (GDScript, GL
Compatibility renderer), built Android-first (landscape) with keyboard and gamepad
support for desktop and handhelds such as the Retroid Pocket 5.

You start at a dusk-lit surface outpost, take a contract, ride the lift into a
seeded three-stratum mine, carve your own tunnels, crack ore seams, survive
crawlers, sentries, gas pockets and crumbling ceilings, take the relic from a
sealed ruin vault, then ride a lift out and spend the credits on upgrades.

Everything in `assets/` (pixel art, parallax, sound effects, ambient music) is
generated by the code in `tools/assetgen/`. The only third-party files are two
SIL Open Font License fonts (see *Credits*).

## How to play

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D, arrows | Left stick, D-pad | Left floating joystick |
| Aim drill up/down | W / S | Stick up/down | Push the joystick up/down |
| Jump / air jet / wall jump | Space, Z | A | JUMP |
| Drill (hold) | J, X, Shift | RB / RT | DRILL (hold) |
| Throw charge | K, C (hold down to drop at your feet) | LB / LT | CHARGE |
| Interact (lift, relic, cache) | E, F | Y / X | USE (shown when something is in reach) |
| Pause / map | Esc, P / M, Tab | Start / Back | II button |

* **Drill** hits only the cells touching your body (with aim assist and corner
  brackets that show the targets). It builds **heat**; when the heat bar fills, the
  drill vents and locks until it has cooled to 35%.
* **Sediment** breaks in a few ticks and **basalt** takes several times longer.
  **Ruin alloy** needs the Mk.IV drill and is immune to blasts. **Bedrock** is
  permanent. A **cracked wall** is weak rock hiding a secret.
* **Ore** glows. Breaking a seam spills chunks, which fly to you while you have
  cargo space. Each cell pays out once only.
* **Charges** show a pulsing red blast ring and a fuse that beeps faster as it
  burns down. A blast destroys weak terrain, hurts enemies, and ignites gas. It
  hurts you too, but at reduced strength and with invulnerability frames.
* **Gas sacs** (yellow-green, shimmering) hiss and show a warning ring for 1.3 s
  when broken, then release a lingering toxic cloud. Any blast ignites a vent or
  cloud into a fireball, so you can clear one from a safe distance with a charge.
* **Loose rubble** (pale gravel) falls once whatever holds it up is removed. It
  shakes and drops dust first, then falls: it stuns and damages anything
  underneath, and lands as diggable rubble.
* **Extract** at the surface lift, or at the relay lift in the Spore Veins once
  you have reached and activated it. If you die or abandon the run, your
  unbanked cargo is lost. Upgrades always persist.

### The loop
1. **Outpost**: walk to the *Upgrade Terminal*, the *Contract Board*, the
   *Supply Locker* (buy extra charges) and the *Lift*.
2. **Mine**: three strata. Each has its own palette, terrain, background props,
   lighting and ambient track.
   * **Dustworks**: ochre sediment, timber shaft, roots, fossils, an unstable
     miner camp and a fossil grotto behind a cracked wall.
   * **Spore Veins**: teal caverns, glowing fungus, moss, gas sacs, the relay
     lift and a sealed cobalt vault with a supply cache.
   * **Forgotten Engine**: violet slag, alloy ruin halls guarded by sentries,
     aetherite, and the relic chamber.
3. **Take the relic**. The ruin wakes: an alarm pulses noise through the mine
   every few seconds, so crawlers converge on you. Get to a lift.
4. **Results**: banked ore, relic bonus, depth, time, kills and the seed.

## Running, testing, exporting

Requires **Godot 4.6.x** (developed and tested with 4.6.3 stable).

```bash
godot --headless --import          # first run / after regenerating assets
godot                              # play
./tools/run_tests.sh               # all automated suites (non-zero exit on any failure)
```

`run_tests.sh` imports the project, then runs each suite in its own process. It
fails on any failed check, any `SCRIPT ERROR`/parse error, a timeout, or a
missing result line. Use `GODOT=/path/to/godot`, `TEST_TIMEOUT=<s>` and
`SEEDS=<n>` to override the defaults. Logs go to `build/test-logs/`.

| Suite | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `terrain` | hit counts by material and drill tier, alloy and bedrock immunity, protected cells, ore paying out exactly once, dirty-chunk locality (1 chunk normally, exactly 4 on a corner), edge masks, interior depth, JSON round-trip, grid line-of-sight |
| `save` | progress round-trip, a corrupt file falling back to defaults, hostile values clamped, purchases, settings sanitising |
| `ui` | touch buttons never overlap each other or the HUD across 5 aspect ratios × 3 button scales |
| `gen` | 60 seeds × all contracts: validated route spawn→relic door→lift and depths→relay **without digging**, open standing spawn, no sealed pockets, protected alloy relic shell, crawlers never in rock and under the cap, no ore in protected cells, determinism, zero repair fallbacks |
| `game` | live mine: player lands safely; drilling down removes the floor and the player drops into the hole; ore collected; the same cell never pays twice; collision rebuilt; blasts spare alloy and bedrock but clear sediment; rubble is telegraphed and never lands inside the player; the gas warning comes before the cloud and a blast ignites it; crawler investigates noise, chases on sight, never enters rock, loses interest, is stunned by rubble; sentry warns, fires, goes dormant, dies when its power module dies; extraction banks credits; a new run starts clean; death banks nothing; 900-frame random-input soak |

Last local run (Linux, Godot 4.6.3, headless):
`terrain 20/20, save 11/11, ui 15/15, gen 1130/1130 (0 repairs), game 39/39`.

Other tools:

```bash
# colour-coded PNG of a generated mine (+ reachability overlay)
godot --headless res://tests/gen_dump.tscn -- seed=123 out=/tmp/map.png
# in-game screenshot harness (needs a display, e.g. xvfb-run)
xvfb-run -a godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 res://tests/shot.tscn -- screen=mine seed=7 frames=120 out=/tmp/s.png
# regenerate all art + audio (Python 3, numpy, Pillow)
cd tools/assetgen && python3 gen_tiles.py && python3 gen_sprites.py && python3 gen_bg.py && python3 gen_audio.py
```

### Android
The `Android` preset exports an arm64-v8a debug APK (`com.ruindigger.game`,
no gradle build, no permissions, no ads, no network).

```bash
./tools/configure_android_editor.sh <ANDROID_SDK> <JAVA_HOME> <debug.keystore>
godot --headless --export-debug "Android" build/android/ruin_digger.apk
```

You need the Godot 4.6.3 export templates, an Android SDK with `build-tools`
(35 or 36), JDK 17+, and a debug keystore, e.g.
`keytool -genkeypair -keystore debug.keystore -storepass android -alias androiddebugkey -keypass android -keyalg RSA -validity 10000 -dname "CN=Android Debug"`.
`rendering/textures/vram_compression/import_etc2_astc=true` is already set.
Release signing is deliberately not configured: use your own keystore, and
never commit it.

**CI** (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) runs every test suite, then exports and
verifies a debug APK and a Linux build and uploads both as workflow artifacts.

## Architecture

```
src/
  autoload/   balance.gd (all tuning) settings.gd progress.gd (save) audio.gd input_setup.gd game.gd (screen flow + run state)
  terrain/    cell.gd (material ids) terrain_grid.gd (authoritative model) terrain_view.gd (chunk rendering + physics)
  gen/        mine_generator.gd route_check.gd (traversal proof + pocket check)
  player/     player.gd
  enemies/    crawler.gd sentry.gd (+ power module, bolts) grid_path.gd
  hazards/    hazards.gd (charges, gas system, ceiling collapse)
  world/      mine.gd (run scene + shared rules) entities.gd (pickups, lifts, relic, door, caches, lamps) outpost.gd
  ui/         hud.gd minimap.gd touch_controls.gd pause_menu.gd settings_panel.gd title_screen.gd results_screen.gd ui_kit.gd
  fx/         fx.gd (pooled particles + flash lights) shaders, sprite_db.gd
tests/        test_runner.gd, gen_dump.gd, shot.gd
tools/        run_tests.sh, configure_android_editor.sh, assetgen/*.py
```

**Terrain.** `TerrainGrid` is the single source of truth. It holds material, hp,
ore, flags (explored, protected, dug, claimed, no-rubble) and stratum as packed
arrays, and serialises to JSON. `TerrainView` derives everything else from it:

* Cells are 16 px, grouped into 16×16 chunks. An edit marks dirty only the
  chunks within its shading reach (normally one chunk, at most four), and only
  those chunks redraw.
* Collision per chunk is a single `PhysicsServer2D` static body built from
  greedy-merged rectangles plus one-way platform slabs. It is rebuilt only for
  chunks whose solidity changed, at the start of the next physics step. There
  are no per-cell nodes and no RigidBodies.
* Tiles come from a 1024² atlas: 13 material/stratum blocks × 16 edge masks × 16
  seamless texture windows. Cracks for three damage stages are overlaid, along
  with ore and gas sprites, back walls for dug tunnels, and hash-placed
  decoration. Interior cells are shaded by their distance to open space.
* Emissive things (ore glow, fungus, alloy circuits, sentry eyes, gas, sparks,
  fire, pickups) are drawn in an additive layer that sits outside the
  `CanvasModulate` darkness, so they read as light sources. Real `PointLight2D`s
  are used sparingly: the headlamp, lamps, lifts, the relic and explosions.

**Generation** (`MineGenerator`) is seeded and order-sensitive:
1. base rock and wavy strata boundaries
2. stratum-specific caves, cellular-automata smoothing, and filling tiny pockets
3. the protected lift room, then the timber shaft with a ladder and platforms
4. the relay lift station
5. alloy ruin halls with sentries and power modules
6. the relic chamber: a protected alloy shell with one door and a protected
   approach
7. set pieces: the fossil grotto behind a cracked wall, the cobalt vault, and the
   collapsed miner camp
8. the guaranteed route, carved **after** every destructive pass, with ladder
   shafts and plank bridges over caves; secret passages never overwrite route
   cells
9. loose ceilings, ore and gas, placed only on rock and never on the route
10. crawlers
11. validation with `RouteCheck` (walk, fall, 3-cell jump, ladders, platforms;
    no digging, jet or wall jump) in both directions. A repair pass exists but
    the 60-seed sweep asserts it is never needed.

**Collapse rule.** When a cell opens, the cell directly above it is checked. A
LOOSE cell with nothing below it is queued with a 0.75 s telegraph, then becomes
a falling block that moves cell by cell. If it touches the player or an enemy it
shatters and deals damage or a stun. It also shatters instead of landing in
no-rubble zones (lift rooms, route ladders, the relic approach) or inside any
body. A rolling cascade budget (18 per 1.5 s) and a cap of 28 falling blocks
bound the cost. Rubble is always diggable, so a collapse can never permanently
block a route.

**Enemy rules.**
* *Burrow Crawler* moves cell to cell along open cells that touch terrain:
  floors, walls and ceilings. It patrols near home. It hears drilling within 8
  cells and blasts within 16, and investigates. It chases on grid line-of-sight
  within 9 cells, re-planning a bounded BFS (radius 22, ≤1600 nodes) at most
  every 0.45 s. With no open path it slowly chews sediment toward you, never
  basalt or alloy. It gives up after 4 s without sight or fresh noise, and
  sleeps (2 Hz updates, no pathing) when more than 34 cells away. Rubble stuns
  it.
* *Ruin Sentry*: DORMANT (cyan) → sees you within 11 cells → WARN (0.9 s amber
  pulse and beep) → FIRE (a 3-bolt burst at 150 px/s) → COOLDOWN → re-warn while
  it can still see you, or dormant again after 2 s out of sight. Its cable leads
  to an exposed **power module**. Destroying the module, the sentry itself, or
  dropping rubble on it disables it. Bolts stop on terrain and chip sediment.

**Balance.** Every number lives in `src/autoload/balance.gd`: movement (coyote
0.1 s, jump buffer 0.12 s, jump height ~3.3 cells, jet burst, wall jump), drill
power and heat per level, material hp and tiers, ore values, cargo, hull, upgrade
costs, charge, gas, collapse, enemy awareness and timers, map size, strata and
contracts.

**Save data.** `user://progress.json` (credits, upgrades, packed charges, stats)
is written through a temp file and validated field by field on load.
`user://settings.cfg` holds volumes, screen shake, reduced flashing, low
effects, a 30 FPS cap, the FPS counter, and the touch mode, button size and
opacity. The current seed appears on the pause and results screens.

## Manual checklist (needs real hardware)

These have **not** been verified on a phone or handheld. Please check them on
device:

- [ ] APK installs and launches on a Galaxy S25+ class phone and on the Retroid
      Pocket 5. The game is landscape-locked and the HUD sits inside the safe
      area, clear of the camera cutout.
- [ ] Holding MOVE + JUMP + DRILL with three fingers works without any control
      dropping out. Releasing a finger releases only its own control.
- [ ] Button size and opacity sliders take effect immediately. The controls
      don't cover the drill target or the minimap.
- [ ] Gamepad: left stick and D-pad move, A jumps, RT drills, LB/LT charges, Y
      interacts, Start pauses, and menus can be navigated by focus.
- [ ] Frame rate at 60 FPS on the S25+, and 30-60 FPS on the RP5. Test with *Low
      effects* and the *30 FPS cap* both on and off. Turn on *Show FPS* in
      settings to read it.
- [ ] Backgrounding the app pauses the run. On resume nothing is stuck pressed.
- [ ] Every menu (upgrade terminal, contracts, settings) is readable and
      scrollable on a small phone.
- [ ] A full loop (outpost → relic → extract → buy upgrade → next contract)
      takes roughly 10-20 minutes.

## Known limitations
* **No on-device testing yet.** Nothing has been measured on a phone or the RP5,
  including FPS, touch feel and thermals.
* The locally built APK was signed with Google's `apksig` 2.3.0 library through
  a small `apksigner`-compatible shim, because the Android SDK download host is
  blocked in the build sandbox. It is v2-signed (verified), arm64, minSdk 24. CI
  signs with the real SDK `apksigner`.
* Crawler pathfinding is grid-based and deliberately simple. Crawlers can get
  stuck behind basalt; that is intended, and they lose interest.
* Collapse only applies to LOOSE material, so there is no general structural
  simulation, by design.
* No music beyond the four generated ambient drones. Audio is synthesised and
  intentionally lo-fi.
* No mid-run save: a run is a single session. Upgrades and settings persist.
