# Ruin Digger 0.5 — Game Plan

**For:** the coding agent implementing 0.5
**Base:** branch `ccr-79c19570-j1j312` @ `1ccf4c0` (v0.4.0, Generation v2). Read
`AGENTS.md`, `README.md` and `docs/generation-v2.md` first.
**Status:** plan only. No code from this plan has been written yet.

---

## 0. Goals in one paragraph

Make Ruin Digger feel like a **digging** game, not a ladder-walking game.

* The player cuts most of their own paths.
* A new ability builds ladders from rubble energy.
* The camera sits closer to the action.
* The world grows sideways: after the first two zones a bedrock floor forces a
  long trip to the far left or far right, and each side opens into a
  different biome that continues downward.
* Touch controls become configurable (stick size, fixed or floating stick,
  d-pad).
* The minimap opens into a full-screen, zoomable, scrollable map of where the
  player has been.
* A floating-lift bug gets fixed for good.

---

## 1. Ground rules (unchanged from previous work)

* Godot 4.6.3, GDScript (typed, tabs), GL Compatibility, Android first.
* `TerrainGrid` is the only mutable terrain state. Tunables go in `Balance`.
  Generated art and audio come only from `tools/assetgen`. No AI image
  generation, and no paid services.
* Generation stays deterministic per seed and contract.
* `./tools/run_tests.sh`, the `water_live` suite and the `gen_report` sweep
  stay green, with **0 repairs**.
* Never claim a test, benchmark, FPS figure or device run that did not happen.
  Render maps and take screenshots before calling something "visually
  checked".
* Commit in phases. Each phase leaves the game playable and the tests green.

**Invariants that change on purpose in this plan**

* The map is no longer fixed at 72×214 (Phase 4).
* "The route is traversable without digging" becomes "the route is
  traversable with the drill and the ladder ability within budget"
  (Phase 3). Its replacement must be **at least as strict about softlocks**.

## 2. Phase 1 — Floating relay lift (bug, do first)

**What the player saw:** in the Spore Veins (146 m) the relay lift cage hung
in mid-air above a plank walkway.

**What was measured:** generating 240 mines (60 seeds × 4 contracts) on
`1ccf4c0` showed missing floor cells under the relay cage (relay.x ± 2,
row relay.y + 1) in **18 of 240 mines (7.5%)**. Examples (seed/contract
index):

* `153243/1`
* `265891/2`
* `370620/3`
* `80190/0`
* `216595/1`
* `143542/0`

**Cause (from reading the code; find the exact pass with `RD_CELL`):**

* `MineGenerator._relay_station()` fills the floor row with `HARD`.
* It does not mark that floor protected, reserved, as a support or sealed.
  Only the air above it is reserved.
* So later passes can remove the floor: route legs (A* treats it as ordinary
  rock), `_carve_corridor`, branch tunnels (`tunnel_ok` only rejects
  reserved, support, sealed and similar cells), and possibly hanging
  boulders, loose ceilings, water or erosion.
* Meanwhile the `Lift` entity is pinned to `(x0 + 7, y_floor)` and draws its
  cage above that cell. Nothing re-checks that it still stands on anything.

**Fix**

1. In `_relay_station()`, flag the floor row `F_PROTECTED` and add it to
   `support`. Block it in `_build_leg_costs`. Do the same for the surface
   lift room floor and any future lift or station.
2. Add a generator invariant and test: every `lift` entity has
   * a solid, protected floor under its full cage width,
   * 3 cells of headroom, and
   * reachability from the route in both directions.

   Check it for every seed in the `gen` suite.
3. Runtime safety net: if a lift's floor cell ever becomes non-solid
   (blasts, erosion), either refuse that damage (protected) or rebuild it
   immediately. Protected is the simpler choice.
4. Trace one of the listed seeds with
   `RD_CELL=x,y godot --headless res://tests/gen_dump.tscn -- seed=153243 contract=1 out=/tmp/m.png`
   and name the pass in the commit message.

**Acceptance**

* The 240-mine check reports 0 broken lift floors.
* The 500-combo `gen_report` still reports 0 invalid or repaired.

## 3. Phase 2 — Quick feel wins (independent of each other)

### 3a. Camera zoom

* **Today:** viewport 640×360, 16 px cells, `stretch=canvas_items`,
  `aspect=expand`, and no `Camera2D.zoom` is set (`src/world/mine.gd`). A
  20:9 phone sees about 50×22 cells, which is two thirds of the map's width.
* **Change:** add `Balance.CAMERA_ZOOM` (start at 1.4, which shows about
  36×16 cells on 20:9). Add a **Settings → Camera zoom** slider (1.0–1.8) that
  is saved and clamped in `settings.gd`.
* Look ahead about 25% in the direction of movement or drill aim, and smooth
  it, so a closer camera doesn't hide what's ahead. When falling, look
  further down.
* Recheck the HUD, touch buttons, minimap and toasts at every zoom.
  Particles, lights and the water renderer must scale correctly. The water
  density buffer is sized to the viewport, so verify it.
* **Tests:**
  * The UI layout suite runs at zoom 1.0, 1.4 and 1.8.
  * Screenshot harness shots at each zoom on 16:9 and 20:9.

### 3b. Touch controls

Files: `src/ui/touch_controls.gd`, `settings_panel.gd`, `autoload/settings.gd`.

New settings, each saved, sanitised and covered by the save suite:

| Setting | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `move_control` | `stick_fixed`, `stick_floating`, `dpad` | `stick_floating` |
| `stick_size` | 0.6–1.8 (radius multiplier, separate from button `touch_scale`) | 1.0 |
| `stick_deadzone` | 0.05–0.35 | 0.15 |
| `control_opacity` | 0.3–1.0 | 0.8 |
| `dpad_size` | 0.6–1.8 | 1.0 |

* **Fixed stick:** works like today, sized by `stick_size`.
* **Floating stick:** touching anywhere in the left movement zone spawns the
  stick's centre under the finger.
  * The zone is the left half, minus the HUD, minimap and pause rects; reuse
    `occupied_rects()`.
  * When the finger drags beyond the radius, the base follows it, so input
    never saturates and gets stuck.
  * The stick fades out on release. Show a faint "ghost" in the resting spot
    so new players see where to touch.
* **D-pad:** 8-way, with diagonals so drilling up-left and down-right work.
  * Up and down set drill aim just as the stick does.
  * Hold a direction to repeat, with no tap-to-dash.
  * It must feed the same `_set_axis` path, so player code doesn't change.
* **Live preview** in the Settings panel: show the controls at their real
  size and position, and let the player drag the fixed stick and the buttons
  to reposition them (store normalised positions). Add a "Reset layout"
  button.
* **Multitouch:** the move control and the action buttons must work at the
  same time (jump while moving, drill while aiming). Track finger index per
  control.
* **Tests:**
  * The UI suite runs across 5 aspect ratios × 3 sizes × 3 modes with no
    overlaps.
  * Synthetic touch events check that a floating stick spawns at the touch
    point and only inside the zone, that the d-pad gives the right axes for
    all 8 directions, and that two-finger move + jump works.

### 3c. Full-screen map

* Tapping the minimap (or pressing `M` or Tab) opens a full-screen map.
  The game **pauses** while it's open (decision D3).
* It shows explored cells only (`F_EXPLORED`), drawn from the same texture
  `Minimap` builds. `minimap.gd` already has a `full` mode; extend it.
* Pinch to zoom (1×–8×), drag to pan, double-tap to zoom in, a "centre on
  me" button, and a close button. On desktop: mouse wheel, drag, and
  Esc/M to close.
* Markers for: player, lifts (active and dormant), relic (once seen), relay,
  caches seen, landmarks discovered (name on tap), and water bodies seen.
  Add a legend.
* With the wider world (Phase 4), show zone names and boundaries. The
  bedrock shelf should be obvious on the map.
* Performance: one texture, updated incrementally, as today. No per-cell
  draw calls when zoomed out.
* **Tests:**
  * Opening and closing restores the pause state.
  * Pan and zoom clamp to the map.
  * A screenshot at 1× and 4×.

## 4. Phase 3 — Dig-first traversal + Rubble Ladder ability

### 4a. Rubble Ladder ability

**Fantasy:** broken rock isn't wasted. The drill packs rubble into a
**scaffold charge** that you spend to materialise a ladder.

**Energy**

* Breaking cells fills a `scaffold` meter. Suggested amounts per cell:
  sediment 1, loose rubble 1, basalt 2, ore-bearing +1. Alloy and bedrock
  can't be broken, so they give nothing.
* Capacity starts at about 40 units. Add an outpost upgrade line,
  "Scaffold Hopper": 40 → 70 → 110.
* The meter refills to full at the start of each run.

**Use**

* A new **LADDER** touch button and keyboard key (`L` / `Q`).
* The ladder starts on the column of the cell the player occupies (their
  feet cell) and fills **upward** until the first solid cell.
* Length is unlimited by design; only energy and a sanity cap of
  `Balance.SCAFFOLD_MAX_LEN` limit it (the cap is for the visual effect and
  performance).
* **Cost:** 1 unit per cell, plus 2 to activate (decision D2). If energy runs
  short, build as many cells as you can afford, starting at the bottom, and
  flash the meter.
* One-way platforms don't stop it. Water doesn't stop it either (a ladder in
  water is fine). Existing ladder cells cost nothing.
* Build animation: the ladder rises cell by cell at about 60 cells/s, with a
  rubble-compaction particle and sound.

**Terrain**

* Ladders are `Cell.LADDER` with a new flag `F_PLAYER_BUILT`.
* They are non-solid, so water and falling rubble pass through as they do
  with ladders today.
* Drilling one removes it with no reward and no refund.
* Collapses and boulders that land on one destroy those cells. Treat it like
  generated ladders, which are already non-solid.

**Edge cases to test**

* Building while standing on a platform, while on an existing ladder, while
  swimming, while mid-jump, and under a 1-cell ceiling (builds 0–1 cells;
  must not soft-lock or eat energy for nothing).
* Building inside a protected structure: allowed, because the ladder is
  non-solid. It must never replace a solid or protected solid cell.
* Saving and loading mid-run, if runs persist.

**HUD and controls**

* Add a SCAF meter under CHRG.
* The button greys out when the meter is empty.
* The touch layout tests must include the new button.

### 4b. Fewer generated ladders, more digging

* **Keep the authored ladders:**
  * the surface lift room and the Dustworks timber shaft (tutorial zone)
  * relay and station ladders
  * ladders inside ruins and landmarks, where they tell a story
* **Remove automatic ladder shafts** from route legs below the Dustworks.
  `_carve_between_axis` vertical segments currently place a full ladder.
  Replace them with:
  * natural climbable geometry: ledges no more than one jump apart, which
    `RouteCheck` already models
  * short **dig gaps**: plugs of 1–4 soft cells that the player is clearly
    meant to drill, with ore hints or lamp light on the far side
  * deliberate vertical sections where the expected answer is a Rubble
    Ladder
* **Target metrics**, added to `GenMetrics` and the sweep report:
  * ladder cells below the Dustworks reduced by at least 80% versus v0.4.0
  * share of route cells requiring digging: 15–35%
  * the number of vertical sections longer than the jump height that need
    the ladder ability
* **Tutorial ramp:**
  * Dustworks: generous ladders, with a prompt the first time the meter
    fills.
  * Spore Veins: a few ladders, and the first forced Rubble Ladder moment.
  * Deeper zones: mostly self-made paths.

### 4c. Replace "no-dig traversal" with a budgeted fairness check

This is the most important correctness work in the plan.

* Extend `RouteCheck` to a cost model: walking and falling are free; digging
  a cell costs its drill time by material and starting drill tier; climbing
  a ladder segment you build costs scaffold energy.
* Energy is earned by the cells you dig. Simulate it conservatively: only
  count energy from cells dug on the path, not from optional mining.
* **Invariant A (completable):**
  * There is a path spawn → relic door → an active lift that uses starting
    gear only.
  * Dig time on that path stays at or below `Balance.FAIR_DIG_BUDGET`.
  * The ladder energy needed never exceeds what has been earned by that
    point plus the starting capacity.
* **Invariant B (no softlocks):**
  * From every reachable standing cell there is a way back up to a lift
    using the drill and staircase digging alone, with zero ladder energy.
  * Exceptions are pits walled by alloy or bedrock, and those must contain
    an authored exit.
  * Verify it on a sample of cells per seed (a full check may be too slow).
* Unbreakable materials (alloy, bedrock, protected) must never seal a pit
  from above without an authored exit. Add this to the `gen` suite.
* Keep `0 repairs` in the 500-combo sweep. The repair pass may now place a
  ladder or open a dig gap, but the sweep must show it is never needed.

## 5. Phase 4 — Wider world: bedrock shelf and two biome wings

### 5a. Shape (recommended; confirm decision D1)

```
              +------------- surface / outpost -------------+
              |  Zone 1  Dustworks      (about 72-90 wide)  |
              |  Zone 2  Spore Veins                        |
 +------------+==== BEDROCK SHELF (unbreakable floor) ======+------------+
 |  far-left passage down          |          far-right passage down     |
 |  LEFT WING                      |                      RIGHT WING     |
 |  Zone 3L  e.g. Drowned          |    bedrock divider   Zone 3R  e.g.  |
 |           Aqueducts             |    (optional thin    Crystal        |
 |  Zone 4L  ...                   |    crossings deep)   Hollows        |
 |                                 |                      Zone 4R ...    |
 +---------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
```

* Zones 1–2 sit in a central column. Their floor is a continuous
  **bedrock shelf**; break it on purpose only at its far ends.
* The shelf opens into a much wider lower world (about 160–200 cells wide).
  The player must cross the full length of the shelf's underside
  corridor, or the band just above the shelf, to the far left or far right
  to find the way down.
* That horizontal trek is a level in its own right: a long gallery with
  hazards, caches and a mid-way relay lift so extraction stays possible.
* The left and right wings are **different biomes**, each with its own:
  * material palette and tile art (new atlas blocks in `gen_tiles.py`)
  * hazard focus
  * landmark set and chamber archetypes
  * enemy mix and parallax background
* The wings may meet at the bottom in a shared Forgotten Engine vault, or
  each may hold its own relic (decision D1).
* Grid size grows from 72×214 (15k cells) to about 180×320 (58k cells).

**Biome suggestions** (pick two; each must use mechanics the game already
has, so the scope stays sane):

| Biome | Signature mechanic | Already supported by |
|---|---|---|
| Drowned Aqueducts | Pressurised water, floodgates, currents, buoyant debris | WaterSim, floodgate landmark, debris |
| Crystal Hollows | Brittle crystal that shatters into falling shards, geodes, light | Geodes, falling blocks, lights |
| Fungal Depths (deeper Spore) | Gas sacs, spore clouds that block vision, soft and fast digging | Gas system, fog |
| Basalt Foundry | Heat: the drill overheats faster and hot vents pulse; boulders | Heat meter, boulders |

### 5b. Engineering requirements

* **Region-based generation.** Split `MineGenerator` into a world planner and
  per-region generators.
  * The world planner lays out regions: zone bands, the shelf, wings, and
    transition corridors.
  * Each region generator runs today's v2 pipeline with a biome profile:
    rock palette, archetype weights, landmark pool, water/ore/enemy
    budgets.
  * Each region gets its own `GenSpace` and RNG sub-streams derived from
    seed + region id, so one region's tuning doesn't reshuffle another.
  * One global validation runs at the end (Phase 3 invariants, both wings).
* **Transitions** are authored structures with claimed footprints and
  protected floors (learn from Phase 1):
  * shelf-end shafts
  * a relay lift at each wing entrance
* **Performance budgets.** Measure them and report the numbers; don't
  assume.
  * Generation at most 1.5 s on desktop. Measure on a mid-range Android
    device if one is available; otherwise say it was not measured.
  * Chunked rendering and collision must stay incremental. Today's design
    allows this; check that memory and the minimap texture scale.
  * Water sim: only active cells cost time. Profile a flooded wing.
  * `RouteCheck` and the new cost-based check must stay fast enough for a
    60-seed suite in about 2 minutes.
* **Camera, minimap and full map** need world limits, zone labels and the
  shelf drawn clearly.
* **Saves:** the settings and progress formats stay backward compatible. If
  a mid-run save exists, add a version field and reject old run saves
  cleanly.
* **Art:** per-biome tiles, back walls, decor and parallax generated in
  `tools/assetgen/*.py`, then re-imported.

### 5c. Acceptance

* Every seed has both wings reachable, and the shelf is unbreakable except
  at the authored passages.
* The Phase 3 invariants hold for the goal in each wing.
* The two wings differ measurably. `GenMetrics` reports per-region:
  * material mix
  * open ratio
  * water units
  * ladder cells
  * hazard counts
* There is a contact sheet of at least 8 seeds and screenshots of both wings
  and the shelf trek.
* The 500-combo sweep reports 0 invalid or repaired.

## 6. Phase 5 — General generation quality pass

Fable has latitude here. Directions that fit the new dig-first design:

* **Denser rock, fewer pre-dug tunnels.** Natural caves become pockets and
  galleries you break into, not a network you walk through.
* **Discovery beats:** a landmark is visible through a thin wall before you
  reach it, ore veins point toward hidden rooms, light leaks from caches.
* **Readable silhouettes per biome.** A screenshot should identify the zone
  without the HUD.
* **Composed encounters:** a hazard plus a reward plus a choice. For
  example, flood the gallery to kill crawlers but lose the lower cache.
* **Pacing curve by depth:** density of reward, threat and rest areas,
  checked with GenMetrics over the sweep.
* Keep everything from v2: footprint claims, sealed containers, independent
  RNG streams, determinism, 0 repairs.
